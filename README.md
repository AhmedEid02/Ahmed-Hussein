## 🌍 About Me

I am an **Agro-Meteorologist, Climate Intelligence Practitioner, and Geospatial Data Analyst** based in Hargeisa, Somaliland. I hold an MSc in **Agro-Meteorology and Natural Risk Management** from Haramaya University and a BSc in Agriculture from the University of Hargeisa.

My work sits at the intersection of **climate science, early warning, geospatial analytics, agricultural resilience, and operational decision support**. I develop reproducible workflows and digital tools that help turn climate and livelihood information into practical decisions for governments, humanitarian actors, farmers, pastoralists, and local communities.

I am the:

- **Founder of the Community-Centered Climate Resilience Initiative (C3RI)**
- **Founder and Project Lead of Adaptive Action Intelligence (AAI)**
- **Early-Career Researcher and Educator in Climate Science and Agro-Meteorology**
- **Independent researcher working on climate risk, food security, resilience, and geospatial evidence**

## 🚀 Featured Project

### Adaptive Action Intelligence (AAI)

**AAI is an explainable early warning-to-early action platform developed for the IGAD/ICPAC Hackathon 2026.**

It connects the full operational chain:

> Climate and livelihood signals → explainable risk → decision confidence → priority action → responsible actor → expected impact → audience-specific communication

AAI includes:

- **Mission Control** for shared operational awareness
- **Climate Fusion** for transparent evidence interpretation
- **ARIE** for structured climate-risk and decision intelligence
- **AIDA** for executive, government, humanitarian, community, and Somali-language briefs
- **Operational Products** for reports, advisories, coordination notes, and PDF export
- **Open-Meteo, OpenStreetMap, Leaflet, React Leaflet, and Vercel integrations**

## 📌 Selected Work

### Community-Centered Climate Resilience Initiative (C3RI)

A locally grounded climate-resilience initiative focused on translating forecasts into practical and understandable advisories for farmers and pastoralists through community co-design, radio, WhatsApp, youth climate messengers, and local-language communication.

### Climate Information Services and Smallholder Resilience

My MSc research examined how climate information services contribute to smallholder farmers' resilience in Gabiley District, including climate trends, adoption barriers, resilience measurement, and the role of localized information.

### Vegetation and Dryland Monitoring

I develop reproducible workflows for vegetation dynamics, rainfall anomalies, drought indicators, and climate drivers using MODIS, CHIRPS, NASA POWER, Google Earth Engine, R, and GIS.

### Climate-Livelihood Risk and Food Security Analytics

My research uses household survey data, geospatial analysis, and statistical modelling to study climate shocks, food insecurity, coping strategies, livelihood vulnerability, and social protection gaps in Somalia and Somaliland.
